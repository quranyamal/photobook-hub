# PhotoBook Hub Vision

PhotoBook Hub is a web platform that enables customers to create personalized photobooks online and order professionally printed products fulfilled by an in-house production team.

The platform focuses on simplicity, affordability, and quality — owning the end-to-end experience from order to delivery.

Customers upload photos, organize them into albums, customize layouts, place orders, and receive printed photobooks delivered to their homes.

The long-term vision is to become the leading photobook platform in Indonesia, delivering a seamless experience from creation to doorstep.

Success metrics:

* First paying customer
* First successful printed photobook
* 100 completed orders
* Repeat customer rate above 30%
* Positive unit economics per order
