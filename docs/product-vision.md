# PhotoBook Hub Vision

PhotoBook Hub is a web platform that enables customers to create personalized photobooks online and order professionally printed products through production partners.

The platform focuses on simplicity, affordability, and local production partnerships rather than owning printing facilities.

Customers upload photos, organize them into albums, customize layouts, place orders, and receive printed photobooks delivered to their homes.

The long-term vision is to become the leading photobook marketplace in Indonesia, connecting customers, designers, and printing partners through a unified platform.

Success metrics:

* First paying customer
* First successful printed photobook
* 100 completed orders
* Repeat customer rate above 30%
* Positive unit economics per order
